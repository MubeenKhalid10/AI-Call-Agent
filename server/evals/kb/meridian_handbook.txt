Meridian Fleet Systems — Sales Handbook

ABOUT THE COMPANY

Meridian Fleet Systems builds fleet management software for mid-sized logistics
operators. The company was founded in 2019 in Rotterdam and now has 84 employees
across three offices: Rotterdam, Austin, and Singapore. Meridian serves 412
customers who between them operate just over 60,000 vehicles.

The product is sold as a subscription. There is no hardware to buy: Meridian
works with the telematics devices a fleet already has, and ships its own dongle
only for vehicles that have none.

WHAT THE PRODUCT DOES

Meridian has four modules. Live Track shows every vehicle on one map with a
refresh interval of ten seconds. Route Planner builds multi-stop routes and
re-plans them when traffic changes. Maintenance keeps a service schedule per
vehicle and raises a work order automatically when an odometer threshold is
passed. Driver Safety scores each driver on harsh braking, cornering, speeding
and phone use, and produces a weekly coaching report.

Live Track and Maintenance are included in every plan. Route Planner and Driver
Safety are add-ons on the Standard plan and included on Enterprise.

PLANS AND PRICING

Meridian is priced per vehicle per month, billed annually in advance.

The Starter plan is 14 euros per vehicle per month. It covers Live Track and
Maintenance, up to 25 vehicles, and email support. There is no minimum contract
beyond the first year.

The Standard plan is 29 euros per vehicle per month, with a minimum of 25
vehicles. It adds API access, custom reporting, and a named account manager.
Route Planner is 6 euros per vehicle per month on top, and Driver Safety is 5
euros per vehicle per month on top.

The Enterprise plan is 44 euros per vehicle per month, with a minimum of 250
vehicles. Everything is included, along with single sign-on, a private data
region, and a 99.9 percent uptime commitment backed by service credits.

Monthly billing is available on Starter and Standard at a 15 percent premium.
Fleets over 1,000 vehicles are quoted individually.

TRIALS AND ONBOARDING

Every prospect gets a 30 day free trial with no card required, capped at 50
vehicles. Trials can be extended once by 14 days at the account manager's
discretion.

Standard onboarding takes two weeks and is done remotely. Enterprise onboarding
takes four to six weeks and includes an on-site day. Data import from a previous
system is included at no charge for up to three years of history; anything older
is quoted as a project.

SUPPORT

Email support is answered within one business day on Starter, four business
hours on Standard, and one hour on Enterprise. Phone support is available on
Standard and Enterprise from 7am to 7pm Central European Time, Monday to Friday.
Enterprise customers also get a 24/7 emergency line for outages.

The support team is based in Rotterdam and Austin. There is no support coverage
on weekends except on the Enterprise emergency line.

INTEGRATIONS

Meridian integrates with SAP Transportation Management, Oracle Transportation
Management, Microsoft Dynamics, Salesforce, Slack, and Microsoft Teams. Fuel card
integrations exist for Shell, BP and DKV. There is a REST API and a webhook
system on Standard and above; the API is rate limited to 600 requests per minute.

There is no integration with NetSuite today. It is on the roadmap for the second
half of next year but has not been committed to a release.

SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE

Meridian is ISO 27001 certified and completes a SOC 2 Type II audit annually.
Data is stored in the European Union by default, in Frankfurt. Enterprise
customers can choose a private data region in Frankfurt, Virginia or Singapore.

All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Customer
data is retained for 24 months by default and can be configured down to 6 months
or up to 7 years. Meridian has never had a reportable data breach.

CONTRACTS AND CANCELLATION

Contracts run for 12 months and renew automatically unless cancelled 60 days
before the renewal date. There is no early termination for convenience on
Enterprise; Starter and Standard can be cancelled at renewal only.

Meridian offers a 60 day money-back guarantee on the first year of a Starter or
Standard contract. Refunds are not offered on Enterprise, which is instead
covered by the uptime service credits.

COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

The three competitors named most often in deals are Samsara, Verizon Connect and
Webfleet. Meridian wins on price against Samsara, on ease of onboarding against
Verizon Connect, and on the depth of the maintenance module against Webfleet.
Meridian typically loses when a prospect needs in-cab video, which Meridian does
not offer at all.
